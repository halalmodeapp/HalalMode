# Product decisions

Record only approved deviations from Halal Mode's established core here, with a one-sentence rationale for each decision.

- **Push tokens are stored, not only hashed (2026-08-14).** `register_my_notification_device` hashed the token and discarded it, which made every notification unsendable — a push token is the address Apple and Google deliver to, and a hash cannot be reversed into one; the hash is kept alongside for deduplication, the token is readable only by the sending job, and it is deleted the moment a member turns notifications off or closes their account.
- **Location comes from the device, and appearing elsewhere is Premium travel mode (2026-10-03).** Replaces the pending "choose your city" fallback: a picked city let anyone claim to live anywhere, so sign-up now requires device location (with a way back to the setting for anyone who refused), and choosing another city is a Premium feature for a move or a long visit. The check is in the app; the server still accepts any city with its coordinates, so a modified client could place itself anywhere.
- **Sample members are opt-in for testers, with a reset (2026-10-03).** Samples now reach only accounts on `tester_tools` who switch them on (Settings → Manage account), so production behaves like launch for everyone else; testers can also reset their sample rounds and sample connections (`reset_my_sample_flow`, which can only delete rows involving a sample member) to walk the flow again — remove both before launch.
- **Testers are shown sample members (2026-09-29).** With no real members and matching off, every tester finished setup to "No suitable introductions" forever and could try nothing after it; 32 clearly marked sample members (paused, so no real matcher or round generator can ever reach them) are offered through their own `demo_introductions` flag as real rounds on the real path, and they choose back, answer and reply so the whole flow can be tested — turn the flag off before launch.
- **Show Interest replaces pass and pop; Free may choose 3, Premium up to all 10 (2026-10-02).** Members were made to reject every person they did not want before choosing, which is backwards; now they simply choose, swapping out an earlier choice when at the limit, and the others lapse with the set, unseen and untold (migration 0150; open-connection capacity is unchanged).
- **No new set while someone is waiting on you (2026-10-02).** A member whose match has chosen or answered questions, and who has not done theirs, is shown that person's questions instead of a set, and the server refuses a submission until they catch up, so nobody keeps matching while leaving people waiting.
- **Answers can be saved for next time (2026-10-02).** A saved answer only pre-fills the box; the member still reads and sends it themselves, and saved answers are private to them.
- **The project moved to the halalmodeapp account (2026-10-02).** The new Supabase project `ziboxxxiedcqfdgzqgjv` replaced `rikqvwwhuwstanngsihs`; test and sample members were copied with their ids, real accounts were not, and scheduled jobs were repointed (migration 0152).
- **Their answers stay sealed until you have answered all of yours (2026-10-02).** Revealing each answer as soon as both had written it let a member read early answers before writing the rest; now nothing of theirs is shown until every question has your answer (migration 0153).
- **The recap shows both answers side by side and a short written summary, never a count (2026-10-02).** The per-question "aligned" verdicts compared answer text word for word, so they almost always read "0 of N"; the summary is written by Claude from both members' answers when an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret is set (answers are sent to Anthropic for this, which the privacy policy must say), and otherwise built from the profile comparison.
- **Empty conversations show a faint suggested opener instead of buttons (2026-10-02).** Twenty openers in a random order, changing every few seconds; nothing is filled in or sent.
