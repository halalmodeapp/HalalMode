# Product decisions

Record only approved deviations from Halal Mode's established core here, with a one-sentence rationale for each decision.

- **Push tokens are stored, not only hashed (2026-08-14).** `register_my_notification_device` hashed the token and discarded it, which made every notification unsendable — a push token is the address Apple and Google deliver to, and a hash cannot be reversed into one; the hash is kept alongside for deduplication, the token is readable only by the sending job, and it is deleted the moment a member turns notifications off or closes their account.
- **Location comes from the device, and appearing elsewhere is Premium travel mode (2026-10-03).** Replaces the pending "choose your city" fallback: a picked city let anyone claim to live anywhere, so sign-up now requires device location (with a way back to the setting for anyone who refused), and choosing another city is a Premium feature for a move or a long visit. The check is in the app; the server still accepts any city with its coordinates, so a modified client could place itself anywhere.
- **Seven distinct body types, up to two per member (2026-10-04).** Seventeen options were mostly synonyms (Slim, Slender, Lean, Tall and lean; Fit, Athletic, Toned; Broad, Stocky, Sturdy), so members could not tell them apart and a filter had to tick several to mean one thing. Now Slim, Athletic / toned, Muscular, Average, Curvy, Broad / stocky, Full-figured / plus size; a member may pick two (e.g. Athletic and Curvy). The first stays the main answer for matching's closeness scale; a body-type filter is met by either. Old answers and filters were mapped to the nearest new option (migration 0176).
- **Background is two questions: ethnicity (up to 2) and heritage (up to 3 countries) (2026-10-04).** The single ethnicity list, even with 110 specific entries, mixed race, ethnicity, nationality and diaspora identity, listed Muslim minorities instead of whole peoples ("Thai Malay" but not "Thai"), and allowed one answer, so "Lebanese American" or "Black, Nigerian and British" could not be said. Now: up to two broad ethnicities (15, no countries; mixed heritage picks two) and up to three heritage countries from every country in the world (ISO codes, named by the device in any language). Either can be "prefer not to say". Premium filters on either match anyone sharing one answer. No list of specific peoples (it always leaves someone out). Supersedes the next entry (migration 0175; the old single column is kept but unread).
- **Ethnicity lists 110 specific ethnicities and origins under 16 broad groups (2026-10-04).** Fifteen boxes left many members unable to find themselves; each broad group (Arab, South Asian, Black African…) can still be chosen on its own and now lists specific ethnicities and origins under it (Egyptian, Pakistani, Punjabi, Somali, Hausa, Bosniak…), searchable, in all 16 languages, plus a new North Caucasian group. `public.ethnicity_region` is the single list of valid ids and maps each to its group, so a Premium filter on a group also includes members who chose something within it (migration 0174; the app's list is generated from the same source).
- **Introductions start only when the profile and preferences are complete (2026-10-04).** Matching now also requires languages, education, whether you have children, when you would like children, religious dress, ethnicity, your height and body type, and a bio of 80 characters (was 40), as well as name, location, a photo and saved preferences (migration 0173). "When would you like children" has always had a default, so a new `family_goals_answered_at` records that the member chose it; the column itself is unchanged so matching is unaffected. Members see one checklist of plain steps with large ticks, on the profile and on Daily, and finishing everything shows a short celebration with a button that starts their introductions. Existing testers must complete these before they get introductions again; sample members are unaffected.
- **Every member answers the Premium questions, and they show on profiles (2026-10-04).** Has children, when they want children, religious dress, ethnicity, education and languages are required on everyone's profile and shown under "About {name}" with sect, practice and timing, so free members see them and only Premium members filter by them; "prefer not to say" and blank answers are left out. Height, weight and body type stay private, used only by matching (migration 0172).
- **Free and Premium filters (2026-10-04).** Free members filter by age, height, location/distance and sect; body type, practice, marriage timing, children (has children and when they want them), career, languages, education level, religious dress (women's and men's lists) and ethnicity are Premium. A Premium filter only rules people out when its "must have" is on and only for a Premium member; a free member's old must-haves on the newly Premium criteria no longer apply. The tab is now called Preferences.
- **Pre-launch red-team fixes (2026-10-04).** Location is stored rounded to about 1 km (all paths, existing rows included); sets, their photos and selections are unreachable before the member's dawn; both selection routes enforce the answer-debt gate; block, report and hide work without accepting new terms; reports are written only through contextual RPCs, rate-limited, and outlive either account for moderation; a phone's push token belongs to its latest account and sign-out releases it; notification claims are leases with per-token retirement and 12-hour expiry; other members can read only attached media, signed links last 20 minutes, and uploads are capped per account; answers reject contact details; the AI summary is disclosed as the "Halal Mode Bot summary" (provider not named, names not sent); legal documents bumped to 2026-10-04.
- **Three questions each, three to six shared (2026-10-03).** Each member now picks three questions instead of five and every pick is kept, so a pair answers between three and six; each question says who chose it ("Hafsa also chose this question!"), and answering moves straight to the next question with the other person's answers kept for the side-by-side recap.
- **Sample members are opt-in for testers, with a reset (2026-10-03).** Samples now reach only accounts on `tester_tools` who switch them on (Settings → Manage account), so production behaves like launch for everyone else; testers can also reset their sample rounds and sample connections (`reset_my_sample_flow`, which can only delete rows involving a sample member) to walk the flow again — remove both before launch.
- **Testers are shown sample members (2026-09-29).** With no real members and matching off, every tester finished setup to "No suitable introductions" forever and could try nothing after it; 32 clearly marked sample members (paused, so no real matcher or round generator can ever reach them) are offered through their own `demo_introductions` flag as real rounds on the real path, and they choose back, answer and reply so the whole flow can be tested — turn the flag off before launch.
- **Show Interest replaces pass and pop; Free may choose 3, Premium up to all 10 (2026-10-02).** Members were made to reject every person they did not want before choosing, which is backwards; now they simply choose, swapping out an earlier choice when at the limit, and the others lapse with the set, unseen and untold (migration 0150; open-connection capacity is unchanged).
- **No new set while someone is waiting on you (2026-10-02).** A member whose match has chosen or answered questions, and who has not done theirs, is shown that person's questions instead of a set, and the server refuses a submission until they catch up, so nobody keeps matching while leaving people waiting.
- **Answers can be saved for next time (2026-10-02).** A saved answer only pre-fills the box; the member still reads and sends it themselves, and saved answers are private to them.
- **The project moved to the halalmodeapp account (2026-10-02).** The new Supabase project `ziboxxxiedcqfdgzqgjv` replaced `rikqvwwhuwstanngsihs`; test and sample members were copied with their ids, real accounts were not, and scheduled jobs were repointed (migration 0152).
- **Their answers stay sealed until you have answered all of yours (2026-10-02).** Revealing each answer as soon as both had written it let a member read early answers before writing the rest; now nothing of theirs is shown until every question has your answer (migration 0153).
- **The recap shows both answers side by side and a short written summary, never a count (2026-10-02).** The per-question "aligned" verdicts compared answer text word for word, so they almost always read "0 of N"; the summary is written by Claude from both members' answers when an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret is set (answers are sent to Anthropic for this, which the privacy policy must say), and otherwise built from the profile comparison.
- **Empty conversations show a faint suggested opener instead of buttons (2026-10-02).** Twenty openers in a random order, changing every few seconds; nothing is filled in or sent.
