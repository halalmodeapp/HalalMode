# Halal Mode invariant audit

Audited feature/halal-cards at 63c76b6 and the working tree on 2026-11-07.

An invariant here is a property that the product, a downstream reader, or another service relies on. I included checks that are incomplete across entry points, checks that use the wrong evidence, and guarantees that hold only if an operation finishes in a particular order. Each finding gives a counterexample or the missing precondition. **Reproduced** means executed locally. **Source trace** means the relevant control flow or interleaving was verified in source. **Policy** means current behavior is clear and the intended contract needs a decision. **Approved** means the repository records the exception.

I used the manifesto and approved decisions as the product contract. I treated the earlier 94-machine review and its six follow-up reports as a baseline, then traced effective definitions through the later migrations and compatibility RPCs. The deployment notes disagree about which fixes reached production, so the findings describe this checkout.
