# Halal Mode: what this app is

Read this before changing anything. When a metric, a simulation or a best practice disagrees with this page, this page wins. If you must break it, ask the founder first.

Halal Mode is a Muslim marriage app. It exists to get people married and off the app. Everything else on the market is an open market built to keep people scrolling, and that is why those apps fail.

## Why other apps fail, and what we do instead

| On other apps | Here |
|---|---|
| An open market. Everyone chases the same few. Women who are 5s go after the jocks who get all the girls, and average men, and those women, end up empty handed. That is hypergamy, and it is mostly wasted interest. | **Five curated introductions a day, and like meets like.** Nobody browses. People are shown others at their own level. |
| An average woman sees 1000+ likes in a day or two and realises she has been paraded in front of tens of thousands of men. Humble women leave or blur their photos. Who remains is narcissists. The rest come to think all men are disgusting. | **Women are not paraded.** No flood of likes or views. No need to blur a photo to feel safe. A person is shown to a handful of people a day, and never knows a count. |

| Men match, learn her weight, then ghost or trail off. She never learns why, and he will not say it. | **Build and weight are declared and matched on preferences,** so each person knows the other accepts them. Only matching uses them. They are not on the profile. |

| Infinite choice makes people pickier: grass is greener, ego swiping. | **Scarcity is the product.** One set a day. There is always a reason to take this one seriously. |

## The rules of the app

**The day.** Each member gets one set, opening at their own Fajr and lasting until the next. Free: 5 introductions, 3 picks, 5 open connections. Premium: 10, 10, 10. Everyone gets five whenever five permitted people exist. A set that has opened never changes.

**Who sees whom.** Introductions are two-way while seats last. Anyone still short gets one-way cards, least-seen people first, whichever side is crowded. There is no fixed cap, so in a crowded city the most-seen person may be shown to 100 or more people a week, and the founder has accepted that because they never see a count and still see only five themselves. A yes on a one-way card waits up to 7 days and takes an ordinary place in the other person's five. They see it once, and if they say yes it is a match at once. Nobody is told who is waiting. A yes may lapse unseen, so never imply it was seen or rejected.

**Picks** are private. Nobody ever learns they were not picked. Both pick, and it is a connection. If either is at their limit it waits until there is room, and they stop getting new sets while full. A waiting match is shown to both on Connections, photos blurred, with a plain word on why it waits. It survives later preference changes, and ends only on a block, a hide or a deletion.

**A connection** moves through: choosing questions, answering (two each, released to both at once), recap, open. Contact details are filtered until then. Answers owed block the next set.

**Eligibility, all hard rules:** opposite gender, both ready, not paused, consent given, not blocked, not hidden, never connected before, not passed on. Every must-have binds. Both sides have to accept each other.

**Must-haves.** Free members can make age, height, body type, distance and sect absolute. Premium adds practice, marriage timing, children and background (ethnicity, heritage, language, education, dress, diet, where they grew up). Declining to answer is never held against anyone.

**Countries.** Choose as many as you like, and introductions only come from those. None chosen means your own. Your own is an ordinary choice, so you can leave it out. Both people must have chosen each other's country. Wanting someone abroad is not the same as wanting to move, so this has nothing to do with `relocation`.

**Marital status** is declared and shown. Men may declare being married. Women choose whether they are open to that, and the default is no.

**Repeats.** New people come first, then people seen once, then people seen more. A familiar face beats an empty seat, so someone can return as soon as the next day, never the same day. A pass closes the pair for 30 days, two passes close it for good, hiding closes it both ways for good, immediately. While a yes is waiting, that pair is not dealt again.

**Too few.** Five is the promise, and a short set still goes out: three possible means three shown. Someone whose own filters leave them too few is told so, and told which filter costs them the most. Never name a filter if the real cause is the city or who they have already seen. Fewer than three always gets a plain word: few people match your filters and location right now. Launch is city by city, because a small city simply runs out of people.

**Closing a connection** is never a silent disappearance. The one who closes picks a kind reason or writes their own words, and the other person gets a gentle note: "Sorry, I don't think we are a good match: {reason}. I wish you the best."

**Halal Bot** gives a member honest, gentle advice on their own photos, only when they ask, and only they see it. It talks about the photograph and never rates the person. The first look is free and covers every photo. After that each new photo uses one of three free looks, and Premium has no limit.

**Leaving.** Deleting an account asks why, and above all whether they married. Answering is optional and never gets in the way of leaving. The account is held for 30 days, and signing back in can keep it. Withdrawing consent for sensitive data is offered as the same thing, because nobody can be matched without it. The answer keeps nothing that says whose it was, so it outlives the account. It cannot catch someone who only uninstalls, since an app is not told that.

**The liked-you tray** shows free members blurred teasers of who liked them, capped at ten, and Premium unlocks them. The founder has accepted this one-sided exposure: a few are not a flood.

**Premium** sells more of the same honest things: more introductions and picks, more filters, Halal Cards for a couple, travel mode. A Premium member's ten puts them in ten people's sets, which the founder has accepted.

## The score

Every member has a **private desirability score**, 0 to 1, with a confidence (`matching_signal_estimates`). Be plain about it: this is an attractiveness and appeal rating, measured from behaviour, and it is what makes like meets like possible.

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---:|
| How often they are picked | 0.40 |
| How often they are picked first | 0.20 |
| How long people look at their photos | 0.15 |
| Mutual first choices | 0.15 |
| How long people read their bio | 0.10 |
| How long people look at their profile | 0.05 |
| Photo quality from AI (clarity, lighting, framing, background) | 0.10, off for now |

Weights live in `matching_signal_metric_config`, changeable without a release.

**How the matcher uses it.** Every introduction is valued `their fit x your fit x new-face bonus x (need + need) x like-meets-like`. Like-meets-like compares where the two people stand among those at the table, and counts only as far as there is evidence about both. A newcomer is exactly neutral, and at launch, with no evidence, it does nothing. Being wanted never earns anyone more introductions, only people closer to their own level. Its strength is `league_config.like_meets_like`.

**Never** show the score, band, rank, like counts, or one-sided interest to anyone, including the member themselves. No score reaches a client.

## Data and persuasion

We log everything useful for matching, and we are not shy about it: every pick, its rank, every pass and soft select, seconds spent on each profile, bio and photo, photo quality, conversations, closures, outcomes. It is all private, server-side and covered by the privacy notice. Log more if it helps people get matched, and update the notice when you do.

Persuasion is fine and expected: scarcity, a countdown to the next set, pushes about real events, a gated reveal, a good paywall. The line is deception: nothing fake and nothing false. No invented members, likes or countdowns, no hiding how to cancel. That line is commercial as much as legal: this app processes religion and ethnicity for people in the UK and Europe, and it depends on the App Store and Play Store and on women trusting it.

## How the matcher works

A daily league. The pool is dealt onto tables of about 500, each matched and committed alone, so a failure costs one table and another worker retries it. A schedule moves groups between tables, so everyone in a country meets everyone within days. A city under 500 is one table, and every pair is compared. Workers only propose: the database re-checks every introduction against live rules when it commits, again when it is shown, and again when it is picked. Details: `docs/BACKEND_SIMULATION.md`, `src/matching/league.ts`, `supabase/migrations/20261105090000_league.sql`.

## The targets

Five a day for 100% of people is the ideal, and 95% is the bar for cities of about 3,000 and up. The founder's aim is a match on 75% of the days someone is active. No tested option reaches that, so the first working target is a match on about 1 in 4 days someone opens the app. See `docs/FIVE_A_DAY_PLAN.md`.
