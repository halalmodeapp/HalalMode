Delete your Halal Mode account

In the app: open You → Settings → Manage account → Delete account. Your profile is hidden immediately.

Without the app: email hello@halalmo.de from the address you sign in with, asking us to delete your account. We reply within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photographs, voice introduction, preferences, answers and messages are permanently deleted 30 days after your request. If you change your mind within those 30 days, email us and we can stop it.
  • Kept for up to two years: safety reports made by or about you, and the record of an account removed for breaking the terms, so a removed member cannot simply return.
  • Kept as tax law requires (usually six years): payment records, held by Apple or Google.
